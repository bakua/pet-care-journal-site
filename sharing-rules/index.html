Private household sharing

Care together, respectfully.

Pet Care Journal helps trusted people coordinate animal care. These rules apply to records and people within an invited household.

Rules version 2026-10-01 · Effective 1 October 2026

Before you share

Read and accept these rules in the app before creating or uploading shared content. Acceptance permits synchronization of household-connected records. When you connect a local journal to a household, its supported existing individual animal profiles, routines, journal entries and corrections can be uploaded too, and future changes are shared. Individual-animal profile images are shared too. Original selected photos, animal-group photos and journal attachments stay on your device.

Share accurate care records and instructions you are entitled to share. Tell other caregivers when you enter their contact details. Shared records are visible to authorized household members according to their permissions. There is no public feed or chat.

What is not allowed

  • Harassment, hateful or degrading attacks, threats, or encouragement of violence or animal cruelty.
  • Sexual or exploitative content, including any sexual content involving children.
  • Scams, spam, impersonation, or deliberately misleading care records that could put an animal or person at risk.
  • Publishing someone else’s private information without permission, or sharing passwords, sign-in codes, or other secrets.
  • Unlawful content or material that infringes another person’s rights.
  • Abusing access controls to harass another caregiver.

Factual observations and instructions from an animal’s caregiver or veterinarian belong in the care journal. The app records what you enter; it does not provide diagnosis, treatment advice, or dose calculations. Consult a qualified veterinarian for medical decisions.

A concern about household sharing

Contact hello@bakuapps.com if you need help with shared content or household access. Describe the concern without sending passwords, sign-in codes, full exports, care records, photos, or another caregiver’s personal information.

Manage household access

The household owner can choose Revoke access for a caregiver in Backup & sharing. Authorized access to shared care is then revoked. Previously downloaded records can remain on a disconnected device until it reconnects and its access is checked; exported copies cannot be recalled.

You can sign out and remove the household’s shared data from this device using the app’s sharing controls. This does not delete another person’s account or the household’s records. For online account deletion, use the separate account-deletion steps.

Shared care and device reminders

Each device manages its own reminder permissions and alerts. Postponing or silencing an alert does not record care for the household. Use the app’s care actions to confirm completion or skip an occurrence. An offline device can still alert after another caregiver has recorded care until that update reaches it.

Shared text checks

Basic text checks can reject some objectionable shared text. These checks cannot identify every problem. For help with a concern, use the support contact above. The privacy policy explains how support correspondence is handled.
