This notice covers this website and its support contact. The app is preparing for its first public release.

Updated 7 September 2026

Who operates this site

BakuApps operates Pet Care Journal. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@bakuapps.com.

Visiting this website

This is a static website. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, contact forms, or third-party fonts.

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Read GitHub’s explanation of Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement for its handling of that information.

Contacting support

Email links open your email application. Sending an email shares your email address and the information you include with BakuApps and the email services used to deliver the message. We use that correspondence to respond to your request.

Please send only what is needed to explain the issue. You can use the same contact to ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of your support correspondence. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

App privacy policy

The final app policy will be published here before public release.

Production hosting, providers, and backup and log retention are still being finalized. This website notice is not the complete policy for an online Pet Care Journal account.

The release is being prepared with device-local care records and optional household sharing. Photos and attachments are intended to remain on the device where they were added. Account deletion and data export are part of release preparation.

If you have an internal test build, contact support for questions about that build and its data handling.
