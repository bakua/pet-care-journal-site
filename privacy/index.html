Your privacy

Your care. Your records.

This policy covers the Pet Care Journal app, optional online household sharing, this website, support correspondence, and private in-app safety reports.

Effective 30 September 2026; updated 1 October 2026

Postal address: Nebovidy 13, 280 02 Nebovidy, Czechia. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@bakuapps.com.

On your device

You can use the local journal without an online account. Animal profiles and care instructions, routines, observations, care events, local photos and attachments, PDF care reports, and app settings are stored in the app on your device.

PDF care reports, search, and local exports are produced on your device. A care report or export goes to another destination when you choose to save or share it. Photos and attachments remain on the device where you added them in this release.

Notification permission is optional; reminders are managed on your device. You can change device permissions in operating-system settings. Your device or operating-system backup settings may also affect copies stored by your device provider.

When you use household sharing

New sharing accounts use Google or Apple. Existing email accounts may also sign in with a password verified by our authentication service. We receive the provider identifier and available account email and display name needed to operate your account. Google sign-in can also provide profile information such as a link to your Google profile photo and, for a work account, its organization’s domain. That information may be stored with your account. The app does not download the profile photo. The online service stores account and household identifiers, member permissions, shared animal profiles, instructions, routines, care events, notes, corrections, caregiver attribution, and any veterinarian or emergency-contact details you enter, such as names, phone numbers, email and postal addresses, and relationships. It also processes a random app installation identifier and connection, security, and diagnostic metadata. Connection logs can include an approximate location, such as your country or city, inferred from your internet address.

Authorized household members can see the animals and care records shared with them, according to their permissions. A household member may enter your caregiver name or contact details. Only share information you are entitled to share, and let the people concerned know about this policy. The app has no public feed.

Account information and shared records are necessary for online sharing. You can continue using a local journal without providing them to our online service. Do not enter sensitive personal information about people into animal-care notes.

Sharing rules and private safety reports

Before shared content is uploaded, the app asks you to accept the sharing rules. Acceptance permits synchronization of household-connected records. Connecting a local journal to a household can upload its supported existing individual animal profiles, routines, journal entries and corrections; future changes are shared too. Animal groups, photos and attachments remain on-device in this release. We record the accepted version and time with your account so the service can apply the current rules. This agreement is separate from a device permission or a general privacy consent.

Basic text checks on the device and server can reject some objectionable shared text. They are limited checks, not a guarantee that every unsafe record will be identified. They do not add advertising, behavioral analytics, or a new AI service.

If you report shared content or a household user, we receive your chosen reason, optional explanation, account and household identifiers, the target identifiers and a snapshot of the reported record that you can access, or the reported user identifier, plus a report reference, receipt time and status. The private case can also contain relevant operator review and action records, original scoped evidence, resolution information, and any documented hold. The operator uses this information to investigate and act on the concern. Reports are stored privately in Supabase and are accessible to the authorized operator, not posted to household care history. No automatic report email is sent. Do not include passwords, sign-in codes, or unrelated private information.

Jakub reviews the private report queue each business day. Reporting does not itself remove a record or revoke access. A household owner can remove a caregiver’s access using the existing sharing controls; you can sign out and remove shared household data from this device. Read the rules and report instructions for these controls and contact details.

Why we process personal information

  • To provide the account and sharing service you request: performance of our contract with you, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse, record rules acceptance, investigate safety reports, and answer support requests: our legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f). For other caregivers’ information, the interest is coordinating care within the invited household.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and handle statutory privacy requests: Article 6(1)(c).

Device permission is a choice about a device feature; it does not authorize unrelated processing. We do not use personal information for automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

How long information is kept

  • Device records: until you remove them using the app’s data controls or otherwise erase the app’s storage. Copies saved or shared elsewhere follow the destination’s settings.
  • Online account and shared records: while needed to provide your account and household service, until the relevant account or record deletion.
  • Export snapshots: access expires after 15 minutes; the app requests removal after download. A scheduled cleanup runs every minute. Outages or a paused project can delay physical removal.
  • Deletion records: an unconfirmed intent expires after ten minutes. A minimal completion receipt, detached from account and household identity, and minimal deletion synchronization records are retained for 30 days. A committed deletion that has not finished remains pending until it can finish.
  • Private safety reports: while the concern is open, then 90 days after ordinary resolution. The operator removes eligible reports, receipts and private action evidence during business-day queue review. A documented legal or security hold can require longer; access and retained information are limited to that need.
  • Sharing rules and restrictions: accepted rules versions and times remain with your account until committed account-content deletion. A service restriction can retain only your account identifier and restriction time after its report is removed, until the restriction is lifted or your account is deleted. Minimal identifiers that prevent removed shared events from reappearing remain with the household until it is deleted.
  • Support email: ordinary resolved correspondence is deleted from the operator’s mailbox after 90 days. A documented legal or security need can justify longer retention; retained material is limited to what that need requires.

The production service uses Supabase’s Free plan, with one day of visible API/database logs and no included automatic daily database backups. No separate operator database-backup schedule is currently installed. These facts do not establish the lifetime of all provider infrastructure or security records. Provider residual copies follow the applicable provider terms.

Google’s customer terms allow residual copies to be removed as soon as reasonably practicable, within up to 180 days after irreversible deletion, unless law requires retention. The 90-day support-email policy describes our mailbox handling; it is not a promise that every provider copy disappears at that time.

Export, removal, and account deletion

Open Settings, then Privacy & Data, for export and device-data controls. Read the export and device-data instructions.

Your online export includes your rules-acceptance records and your own submitted reports’ reasons, details, targets and receipts. It does not include other people’s reports or confidential operator notes and evidence.

Deleting an owner’s online account removes the whole household and its shared records for all members. Deleting a caregiver’s account removes their account, access, identifying attribution, and authored notes; retained household care history is labelled “Deleted caregiver”. Independently written content from other members remains.

Committed account-content deletion also removes your rules acceptance and unheld safety cases involving the deleted account or whole household, including their private action copies. A documented legal or security hold is the only safety-case exception; information kept under a hold is limited to that need. A held case does not prevent account deletion.

After the server verifies in-app deletion completion, the app erases its local data, photos, reminders, and unsynced changes from the requesting device. Previously downloaded records may remain on an offline caregiver’s device until it reconnects and access is checked. Exports you saved elsewhere cannot be recalled.

You can also request account deletion by email without installing the app. Read the account-deletion steps and what happens next.
