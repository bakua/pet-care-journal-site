These controls are being prepared for the first release. Available options depend on the test build and whether household sharing is configured.

Export your data

Open Settings, then Privacy & Data. Choose Export all app data to create an archive of the data on this device.

In a build with household sharing enabled, Export device and online data also includes a separate authorized online snapshot. An owner receives the shared household record; a caregiver receives their account data and currently accessible care records. Unsynced changes and local photos stay in the device section of the archive.

Choose where to save or share the verified archive. Keep it somewhere private: exports can contain care records and contact details. Archives are machine-readable and cannot currently be imported back into the app.

Remove data from this device

Delete all app data removes app data, local photos, reminders, and unsynced changes from this device. Export anything you want to keep first.

It does not delete an online account, a shared household, data on another device, or copies you previously exported.
