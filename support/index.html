Let’s get it sorted.

Questions about care records, household sharing, or your data? Get in touch.

Jakub reviews the support inbox each business day. Include your app version, device and operating-system version, and a brief description of the issue. Include the non-secret request reference if the app provides one.

Ordinary resolved support email is deleted after 90 days unless a documented legal or security need requires longer retention. Read the privacy policy for provider handling and your rights.

Keep your private information private.

Please do not send passwords, sign-in or invitation codes, tokens, full data exports, pet-care notes, photos, or another caregiver’s personal information.

A concern about household sharing

For unsafe shared content or a household user, open Settings, then Backup & sharing, and choose Report content or a person. The report goes privately to the operator. Read the sharing rules for permitted content, report handling, and household access controls. You can also email the contact above if you cannot report in the app.

Help with your data

  • Export records or remove data from your device
  • Request online account deletion
  • Read the app and website privacy policy

Interrupted online deletion

Reopen the app to check the saved deletion request. If it cannot verify completion, keep the request reference and email support. A missing account or expired receipt alone does not prove that deletion finished.

If offered, Erase this device only is a separate choice: it removes local records, unsynced changes, and saved status access, without confirming or cancelling online deletion. Save your request reference before using it. A deletion already committed on the server continues independently.
